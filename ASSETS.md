# Models and music

All model files are served from this repository. The experience makes no requests to an asset marketplace at runtime.

| Files in `public/models/` | Artist / source | License | Adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `fish1.glb`, `fish2.glb`, `fish3.glb`, `manta-ray.glb`, `whale.glb` | Quaternius, [Animated Fish Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/animatedfish.html), downloaded from the [artist's OpenGameArt upload](https://opengameart.org/content/animated-fish) | CC0 1.0; original notice in `quaternius-license.txt` | FBX converted to GLB with swimming rigs and clips preserved. Repeated material groups consolidated, skin weights normalized, materials converted to physically based materials. |
| `ship-small.glb`, `Textures/colormap.png` | Kenney, [Watercraft Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/watercraft-kit) | CC0 1.0; original notice in `kenney-license.txt` | Original GLB and palette texture. Positioned and rotated in the drifting gardens. |

The model conversion can be reproduced with `node scripts/convert-models.mjs <fbx-directory>`. Extract the Quaternius archive's FBX files into the supplied directory first. Node 22 and the project's installed dependencies are required. The converter writes the five creature GLBs into `public/models/`.

Relics, shells, jellyfish, plants, terrain, ruins, lighting, and interface icons are generated by the project's own geometry and shaders. `src/lib/score.ts` contains the original eight-bar musical progression and melody; `src/lib/audio.ts` synthesizes its instruments, percussion, delay, and reverb in Web Audio. No third-party music recordings or samples are distributed.

For the dense schools, the three fish are baked from their rigged rest poses into shared vertex-colored geometries at startup. Instanced transforms handle swimming paths and local avoidance; a vertex shader animates their tails. Hero manta rays and the whale retain their skeletal swimming clips. Rocks and plant meshes are generated as full 3D forms in `src/world/habitat.ts`.
